About The Initiative

Inclusive Digital Growth for MSMEs

Launched in 2024 by MCCIA, the AI Experience Studio was built on a simple insight: most MSMEs don’t need a lecture on AI — they need a trusted partner to help them apply it.

We started by helping businesses identify where AI could save time, cut costs, and improve efficiency. As our work deepened, it became clear that off-the-shelf tools weren’t enough for every challenge. Today, the studio supports MSMEs end-to-end: from understanding AI’s potential to building and deploying custom applications tailored to their exact needs.

We don’t just advise. We build, test, and deploy — alongside you.
