Foreword

Inclusive Digital Growth for MSME's

Launched in 2024 through the regional MCCIA AI Series, our initiative set out with a clear purpose: to demystify artificial intelligence and make it accessible, practical, and highly impactful for small businesses at the grassroots level.

Following rapid adoption and demand, the chamber officially established the MCCIA Applied AI Studio on June 3, 2025 to provide custom auditing and integration support directly on-ground.

This report stands as proof of what is possible when curiosity and opportunity converge, celebrating 100 small businesses that successfully automated workflows and cut operational costs.

2024 — MCCIA AI Series Launched

Statewide Boot Camps and virtual webinars reaching 1,115 active participants across Maharashtra.

June 2025 — Dedicated Applied AI Studio

Transitioned from general awareness programs to direct, hands-on implementation and technical assessments.

Our Framework

The AI Series Enablement Model

To build sustainable technical capabilities, the initiative guided small businesses through three structured developmental formats:

1. In-Person Boot Camps

Hands-on district training focusing on demystifying core AI logic, mastering basic prompt engineering, and exploring ready-made tools for daily office productivity.

2. Virtual Tech Talks

Regular online peer-to-peer sharing circles where previous boot camp graduates gather to demonstrate their operational workflows and exchange working templates.

3. Tech Pe Charcha

Expert-led, informal discussion labs tracking emerging trends. Features industry veterans like Dr. Navin Kabra and Google AI Consultant Dr. Bhushan Garware.

Roadblocks

Key Challenges Encountered

Upskilling traditional small businesses revealed critical non-technical constraints:

Digital Readiness Gaps

Uneven baseline infrastructure (unstable connections or lack of desktop machines) required significant on-ground personal guidance.

Time Poverty

MSME owners are fully immersed in daily administrative tasks, making time commitment for technology learning the biggest hurdle.

Language Barriers

Technical jargon created initial resistance. Success was achieved by translating prompts into Marathi and local business analogies.

MSME Playbook

Actionable Implementation Rules

Advice compiled from champions who successfully integrated AI workflows:

Start Small, Act Now

Begin by automating email drafts, content proofing, or spreadsheet cleanup. Small wins build early user trust.

Master Prompt Clarity

Better prompts yield better outcomes. Provide context, define roles, and give exact parameters to get practical results.

Human Oversight (Loop)

Verify every output before publishing. AI is a highly performant enhancer, not a total decision replacement.
