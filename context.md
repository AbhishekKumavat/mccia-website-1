# 2. Our Story

## Title

### From AI Awareness to Applied AI Implementation

## Content

The journey began in **2024** with the launch of the **MCCIA AI Series** — an initiative created to help businesses understand the emerging impact of Artificial Intelligence.

The objective was simple:
Make AI practical, accessible, and understandable for MSMEs, professionals, and industry stakeholders.

Over the course of the initiative:

* Sessions were conducted across **14+ districts in Maharashtra**
* More than **1000+ MSMEs** were sensitized to AI adoption
* Businesses were introduced to practical AI tools, workflows, and opportunities
* Industry discussions helped uncover real implementation challenges faced by organizations

As industry interest rapidly evolved from “understanding AI” to “implementing AI,” the need for a dedicated execution-focused initiative became clear.

This led to the launch of the:

# MCCIA Applied AI Studio

### Launched on June 3, 2025

The studio was created to move beyond awareness and focus on:

* AI implementation
* Workflow transformation
* Practical business adoption
* Automation and digitization
* AI-enabled operational systems

Since its launch, the studio has:

* Consulted **650+ MSMEs**
* Conducted implementation-focused workshops and cohort sessions
* Helped organizations identify practical AI use cases
* Supported businesses in improving workflows and operational efficiency
* Guided teams in adopting AI tools and systems in day-to-day operations

Today, the MCCIA Applied AI Studio serves as a platform for practical AI adoption, helping businesses move from exploration to execution.
